Use case: ui-mockup
Create ONE polished production-ready Android mobile app home screen design master for VoiceTodo, a Chinese voice-first personal task/reminder and everyday timer app. This is a fresh product design, not matching any existing code. Single screen only, flat front-on UI, portrait aspect ratio about 0.462 (390 by 844 logical units), high resolution. No physical device frame, no poster, no perspective, no external annotations or moodboard. All UI text in precise simplified Chinese. Refined editorial productivity design with believable compact mobile density, exceptionally readable typography and generous but purposeful spacing. No illustrations.
Visual system: warm near-white #F7F8FA background, white surfaces, ink #182230 typography, secondary #586575, cobalt blue #315BE8 primary action, pale blue #EDF2FF selection, restrained amber #9B5E10 for timer status. Thin #E3E7EE rules. Rectangular cards with 8 logical pixel corner radius, no big shadows. Clean consistent outline icons, no emoji. Use readable sans-serif Chinese typography, title 30 logical pixels, body 16, meta 13. Minimum 48 logical pixel touch areas. Default theme light.

1. Android status bar, time "09:41", normal tiny signal/battery icons.
2. Header horizontal layout: left small date "9月11日 · 星期五", below bold large title "今天". Right two discreet outline icon buttons: search and settings.
3. Brief secondary line "3 件待办 · 1 个计时中".
4. Compact active timer card height about 100 logical units on very pale warm amber surface, subtle amber vertical left border. Left small label "正在计时", title "煮鸡蛋"; right large tabular countdown "06:42" with a small outline pause button in a 48px target. Bottom caption "共 10 分钟" and thin 33% progress indicator. Timer is glanceable and compact, not a giant hero.
5. Section heading "今日安排", small right action "查看全部".
6. Three readable task rows separated by subtle rules on page surface, not three floating cards. Left outlined circular checkbox, center title and supporting time/meta, right small chevron. Fit everything comfortably into one realistic 390x844 equivalent viewport. Maintain consistent 20px side margins, 8/12/16/24 spacing. Strictly no promotional copy, no gamification rings, no avatars, no calendar week strip, no graphs, no giant microphone in center, no floating decorative orbs, no glass blur. Do not add text beyond specified content. Real app screen with clear information hierarchy; timer and task list feel part of one coherent product. This master establishes the reusable typography, navigation, list rows, status card and voice action for subsequent pages.
